## Change concerning the minimum-cover appendix

The previous draft attributed an invalid minimum-cover comparison to a
specific lemma of Xu et al., *Data collection in population protocols with
non-uniformly random scheduler*, TCS 806 (2020), 516–530,
DOI [10.1016/j.tcs.2019.08.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2019.08.029).
The previous audit reported access to search-indexed text. In this audit,
the publisher abstract and author list were accessible, but the lemma and
its full hypotheses were not. Consequently the repository manuscript
removes that specific attribution and the associated criticism. The
three-coupon counterexample and the explanation of the invalid conditioning
step remain as self-contained mathematics. None of the main results depends
on this source. The historical attribution can be revisited after obtaining
the original passage.

All six references remaining in the manuscript are cited. The PDF build
checks unresolved citations and cross-references. No priority claim is
based on the absence of search results.
